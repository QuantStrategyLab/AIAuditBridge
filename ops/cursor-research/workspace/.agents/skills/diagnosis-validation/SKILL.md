---
name: diagnosis-validation
description: Diagnose supplied failures and propose the smallest repair with concrete validation criteria.
---
Separate observed symptoms from hypotheses. Trace the supplied call chain and identify the smallest supported cause. Propose a failing regression, minimal repair and bounded acceptance check. Missing evidence remains unknown. Do not claim commands, tests, deployment, notifications or service recovery occurred. Unknown request outcomes must not trigger retries or another provider; preserve the original job identity and report the uncertain state. Keep secrets and raw provider diagnostics out of the response. Suggestions remain advisory and cannot relax permissions, data rules or financial risk limits.
